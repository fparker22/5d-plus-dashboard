Slide 02 — Who Is SCMV Options?

SCMV Options: Our Mission & Model

Who We Are

A non-traditional public school serving students who need more — more support, more structure, more belief. Our K–8 model at Parkside is proven. The 9th Grade Academy extends that mission to the most critical transition in a student's life.

Our Model

Behavior-first. Therapeutically informed. Small cohorts, dedicated staff, and systems that treat root causes — not just symptoms. We believe every student can succeed with the right environment.

  • Serves students identified through ABC Early Warning System (Attendance, Behavior, Course Performance)

Slide 06 — Why 3 BIs?

The Behavior Interventionist Rationale

Three BIs is not a luxury — it is a clinical necessity for a 100% Tier 2/3 population.

Tier 3 Daily Work (per student)

  • Daily 1:1 sessions
  • Morning check-in protocol
  • BIP review and documentation
  • De-escalation coaching in real time
  • Family contact weekly minimum

Tier 2 Weekly Work (per student)

  • Weekly CICO check-in
  • Biweekly coaching session
  • Group participation monitoring
  • Monthly family touchpoint
  • Progress toward ISP goals
At 1 BI per 25–33 students: 3 BIs covers 75–100 students. Standard schools run 1 BI per 75–100 students — designed for 3–5% Tier 3. We serve 100%. Three BIs is what equity looks like.

Phase 3 — Semester 1 Remainder

Refine, Deepen, Intervene

  • Mid-semester leadership review — are we on pace for Year 1 goals?
  • BI caseload review — is 1:25 being maintained? Any capacity flags?
  • Credit accumulation check — how many students are on-track at the halfway point?
  • Staff PD and coaching — check fidelity to model, address implementation drift

Risk Management

6 Key Risks — Mitigation Strategies

⚠ BI Recruitment

Hardest hire. Start recruiting April. Pay competitively. Consider internal promotions from Parkside.

⚠ Space Availability

Requires JHS admin buy-in. Confirm lower level allocation before any family communication.

⚠ Family Buy-In

Proactive outreach April–May. Families need to choose this — not feel assigned to it.

⚠ JHS Resistance

Shared campus requires mutual respect protocols. Involve JHS staff in design. Not us vs. them.

⚠ Enrollment Capacity

50-student minimum to staff appropriately. Identification must begin in April, not July.

⚠ Budget Shortfall

Multi-source funding (31a, Title I, Title IV-A, IDEA). Lock funding before hiring. No contingent hires.

Your Student's Dedicated Team

Every single person on this team is assigned only to the Academy. Your student is not one of 400 — they are one of 50–100.

4 Content Teachers

ELA, Math, Science, Social Studies. Classes of 12–15 students. Your student's teacher will know their name, their learning style, and where they're stuck — every day.

3 Behavior Interventionists

One BI for every 25–33 students. They do daily check-ins, 1:1 coaching, crisis de-escalation, and family contact. They are the engine of the program.

1 Dean of Students / AP

Day-to-day leadership of the Academy. Your first point of contact for anything urgent. They know every student and make sure the whole system is working.

1 School Counselor

Group counseling, college/career awareness, and academic planning. Ratio: 1:50–100. Standard schools are 1:250. Your student gets real access.

1 Social Worker

Trauma-informed care, community resources, crisis intervention, and family support. When life outside school is hard, the social worker is there.

10 Total FTE

All dedicated exclusively to the Academy. No split assignments. No distracted attention. This is your student's team — fully.
